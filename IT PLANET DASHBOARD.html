WHO WE ARE

Technology with a Purpose.

IT PLANET is a technology-focused initiative built around innovation, creativity and practical problem-solving. We believe technology should make people's lives easier, businesses stronger and communities more connected.

Our work brings together electronics, embedded systems, artificial intelligence and software development. We enjoy understanding technical challenges, exploring new ideas and transforming them into solutions that people can actually use.

WHAT WE DO

Our Technology Services

We combine technical knowledge, creativity and innovation to provide solutions for real-world technology challenges.

Electronic Repair

We diagnose, maintain and repair electronic devices and equipment. Our focus is on identifying faults, restoring functionality and helping customers get more value from their devices.

Embedded Systems

We design smart electronic systems using microcontrollers, sensors, automation and connected devices. From smart parking to automated solutions, we turn ideas into working projects.

AI Prompting

We explore artificial intelligence through effective prompting, creative thinking and practical AI tools to support research, productivity, learning and problem-solving.

Software Error Solutions

We help identify, troubleshoot and resolve software errors, installation problems, system issues and other technical challenges that affect computer performance.

Software Development
